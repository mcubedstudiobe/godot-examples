## How they were chosen

Each source page was opened and its licence read as Creative Commons 0. Where a sound could not be
auditioned it was picked from its page text and measured: duration, level, where its energy sits
in the spectrum and how fast it starts.

- **`machine_thunk.wav`:** no CC0 tennis-ball-machine or air-cannon recording turned up, so it is
  three real recordings summed on their onsets: a hard click for the start, a thump for the body
  and a short spring release. It starts within a few milliseconds and decays steadily.
- **`heartbeat.wav`:** a stethoscope recording that is mostly low frequency with little hiss. The
  quiet gap between the two beats of the pair was cut so the second one lands before the next beat
  at the fastest rate, and a gentle harmonic layer (a saturated copy, high-passed) was added so the
  beat still carries on a phone speaker: the share of energy above 150 Hz went from about 3% to 28%.
- **`ball_bounce.wav`:** one bounce on grass, picked from a recording of about twenty, because a
  soft landing is what a ball makes on a lawn.
